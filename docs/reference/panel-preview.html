    What changed since my first answer

    I said: a hidden webview would break the 60 MB budget.

    What is true: it does not. I measured your own tray apps on this PC, by the same number Task Manager shows. Scribe, with its webview hidden, uses 21 MB. N8 Tray, with no window open, uses 5 MB.

    Why I was wrong: Windows trims a hidden webview. It still reserves about 250 MB on paper (commit), but that is not the Memory column and not what the PRD budget counts.

    So: dropping the webview saves about 16 MB while hidden and maybe 60 to 80 MB while open, and costs a hand-built panel. I would keep the webview.
